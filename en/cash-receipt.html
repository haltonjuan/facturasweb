Cash receipt: what it is, what it includes and a free template

A cash receipt is the proof handed over by the person who receives money, to record that the payment was made. It is used for deposits, advance payments, rent, instalments or small sales, and it helps both the payer and the recipient.

What is a cash receipt and when is it used?

It is a written record that one person handed over a sum of money and another person received it. It is mostly used when the payment is made in cash or by a method that does not produce its own proof, and in situations such as paying down a debt, paying an instalment, a month's rent, a small sale or an advance for a job.

It serves a simple but valuable purpose: if someone later says “I never received that money” or “I already paid”, the signed receipt makes clear what happened. It is a proof of payment; it is not an invoice and does not replace the one your country's tax authority requires.

Details a cash receipt must include

  • Date and sequential number: they let you keep your receipts in order and avoid duplicates.
  • Who pays: name and ID number of the person handing over the money.
  • Who receives: name and ID number of the person receiving it.
  • Amount received: in figures and in words, with the currency if it is not the local one.
  • Description: what the payment was for (deposit, advance, rent, sale, instalment).
  • Payment method: cash, bank transfer or another method.
  • Signature of the recipient: this is what gives the receipt its practical value.

Common mistakes on a cash receipt

  • Leaving the description blank or writing only “payment”, without saying what for.
  • Not stating the remaining balance when the payment is a partial one.
  • Handing it over without a signature or a date.
  • Writing figures and words that do not match.
  • Not keeping a copy, or not numbering the receipts.

Cash receipt, sales receipt and proof of payment

  • Cash receipt: confirms that money was received, whatever the reason.
  • Sales receipt: lists the products or services sold with their prices (see the sales receipt).
  • Proof of payment: a more general record of any payment (see the proof of payment).

Tips for using it well

  • Hand over the original and keep a copy; if you send it by WhatsApp, ask for confirmation that it was received.
  • Never sign blank receipts or receipts with amounts to be filled in later.
  • If the payment was a bank transfer, also keep the screenshot of the bank confirmation.
  • Always use the next sequential number, with no gaps or repeats.

Frequently asked questions

What is a cash receipt for?

It serves as proof that money was handed over and received. It protects both parties if a claim or a doubt about the payment arises later.

Does a cash receipt replace an invoice?

Not necessarily. It is a simple proof of payment. If your activity requires you to invoice, you must also issue the invoice your country requires. Consult an accountant if you have doubts.

Who should sign the cash receipt?

The person who receives the money. If the payer also wants to sign as accepted, they can; what matters is that both parties keep a copy.

Can I make a cash receipt if the payment was a bank transfer?

Yes. State bank transfer as the payment method and, if you like, add the bank or the reference number. That way the receipt and the bank record complement each other.
