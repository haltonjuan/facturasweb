Salary payment receipt: template and required details

A salary payment receipt is the proof an employer gives an employee when paying their salary. It shows how much they earned, what was deducted and how much they actually received.

Here you will see which details to include, how to read a pay receipt, an example with fictional data, the most frequent mistakes and how to create it as a PDF from your phone.

Details a salary payment receipt must include

  • Employer: name and tax ID or ID number.
  • Employee: name, ID number and job title.
  • Period paid: for example, September 1 to 30.
  • Base salary and other payments: overtime, bonuses or commissions.
  • Deductions: health, pension or others agreed.
  • Net amount paid and payment date.
  • Signature of the recipient.

How to read a pay receipt: gross pay, deductions and net pay

  • Gross pay: everything the employee earned in the period (base salary plus other payments).
  • Deductions: the amounts subtracted, such as contributions or agreed deductions.
  • Net pay: what the employee actually receives: gross pay minus deductions.

The actual items, percentages and deductions depend on your country's law and on each contract.

Common mistakes on a salary payment receipt

  • Mixing different periods in one receipt.
  • Not itemizing the deductions.
  • Handing it over without a signature or a date.
  • Not separating overtime or bonuses from the base salary.
  • Not keeping a copy signed by the employee.

What a pay receipt is not

It is a simple proof of payment. It does not replace the official payroll, social security enrollment or your country's labor and electronic payroll obligations. For those matters, consult an accountant or a labor lawyer.

Frequently asked questions

Does a pay receipt replace the official payroll?

Not necessarily. It is a simple proof of payment. Labor and electronic payroll obligations depend on your country; consult an accountant or a labor lawyer.

How do I create one on my phone?

Use the generator, write the description as salary payment, add the period and amounts, and download the PDF.

Do I have to give a receipt every time I pay?

It is good practice to give one with every salary payment and keep a signed copy. The exact requirements depend on your country.

Can I include overtime or bonuses?

Yes. Write them in the description or add them to the amount, and make the breakdown clear so the employee understands how the total was calculated.

Who signs the salary payment receipt?

The person who receives the payment, that is, the employee. The employer may also sign or stamp their copy.
