How to fill out a legal fee statement for freelancers?

For your fee statement to be valid and professional, it should include five key elements: 1) the issuer's full details (your name or business, ID number, phone and address); 2) the details of the client or company you are billing; 3) a unique sequential number to keep your accounting under control; 4) a detailed description of the services provided (avoid vague wording and be specific); and 5) clear bank transfer details (bank, account type, account number and IBAN if international). With facturasonline.lat you can generate your fee statement as a PDF with selectable text, including all of these requirements, for free and in under a minute.

What mandatory details must a cash receipt or informal proof of sale include?

A professional cash receipt must certify the actual receipt of money. The mandatory details to include are: the exact date of the transaction, the receipt or sequential number, the name of the person or business handing over the money, the detailed concept of the payment (e.g. "Full payment of Invoice No. 005") and, most importantly, the payment method used (cash, bank transfer, card, etc.). At the end of the document, a space for the signatures of the issuer and the recipient is essential to give it commercial validity.
