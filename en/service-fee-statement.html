Fee statement for services rendered: example and template

If you work as a contractor or independent professional, a fee statement for services rendered is the document you use to bill your client for the work done in a given period. It should be clear, so that you get paid without delays or rejections.

Here you will see what it should say, how to word the scope and the period, a complete example with fictional data, the attachments that are often requested and how to create it as a PDF from your phone. If you are looking for the general version, see the fee statement.

What a fee statement for services must say

  • Your details: full name, ID number and contact details.
  • The client: name or company name and tax ID or ID number.
  • Issue date and sequential number.
  • Scope and period: the service provided according to the contract and the time span you are billing.
  • Amount due: in figures and in words.
  • Transfer details: bank, account type and account number.
  • Your signature.

How to word the scope and the period

The scope is the description of the service, and it should match what your contract or service order says. The period is the time the charge covers. The more specific they are, the fewer questions you will get before being paid.

  • Better: “Technical support and equipment maintenance services, from September 1 to 30, 2026”.
  • Worse: “Work done”.

Example of a fee statement for services (fictional data)

  • Fee statement No. 009 · Date: October 1, 2026
  • Billed by: Sergio Andrés Vélez, ID No. 80.456.789
  • Client: Fundación Horizonte, Tax ID 800.456.123-1
  • Scope: technical support and equipment maintenance services
  • Period: September 1 to 30, 2026
  • Amount: COP 2,400,000 (two million four hundred thousand Colombian pesos)
  • Payment: transfer to savings account No. 000-000000-00, in the name of Sergio Andrés Vélez
  • Signature: Sergio Andrés Vélez

Sample wording: “Juan Pérez, ID No. 123.456.789, is owed the sum of COP 1,500,000 (one million five hundred thousand Colombian pesos) for graphic design services provided during September 2026.”

Attachments that are often requested

It depends on the client and the country. In some cases they ask, in addition to the fee statement, for proof of social security payment, an activity report or a copy of the contract. Ask whoever hires you what they need, and you will avoid rejections caused by incomplete paperwork.

Common mistakes

  • Billing without describing the service or the period.
  • Forgetting the amount in words or writing figures that do not match.
  • Leaving out the payment details, which delays payment.
  • Using a repeated sequential number.
  • Sending it without the attachments the client requires.
  • Billing a period different from the one your contract covers.

Frequently asked questions

What supporting documents are requested besides the fee statement?

It depends on the client and the country. In some cases they also ask for proof of social security payment or an activity report. Ask whoever hires you which attachments they need.

Can I make one from my phone?

Yes. Open the generator, choose fee statement, fill in the details and download the PDF, ready to send by WhatsApp or email.

How often should I send the fee statement?

Your contract or your agreement with the client sets it: it may be monthly, per delivery or when the work is finished. Confirm the deadline for submitting it and respect it.

How is it different from a regular fee statement?

In its content: the one for services refers to a contract or agreement, states the scope and the period billed, and is often accompanied by attachments. The basic structure is the same.

Is a fee statement for services an invoice?

No. An invoice is a tax document with its own rules. If you are required to invoice, you must issue the one your country requires; if in doubt, consult an accountant.
