Advance payment receipt: free template

When someone pays you an advance for a job or a product, or makes a partial payment toward a debt, it is wise to put it in writing. An advance payment receipt prevents misunderstandings about how much was paid and how much is still owed.

Here you will see when to use it, what it should include, an example with fictional data, which conditions are worth writing down and how to create it as a PDF from your phone.

When is it worth recording an advance payment?

Almost whenever the payment does not cover the full amount. It happens with a tailor making clothes to order, a technician who asks for an advance to buy materials, a photographer reserving a date, a supplier receiving part of the payment before delivering, or a person paying down a debt. In every case, the receipt records how much was paid, for what and how much remains.

What an advance payment receipt should include

  • Date and sequential number.
  • Who pays and who receives: name and ID number of both.
  • Advance amount: in figures and in words.
  • Description: what the advance is for, clearly described.
  • Total agreed amount and remaining balance.
  • Payment method.
  • Signature of the recipient.

Advance payment receipt example (fictional data)

  • Advance payment receipt No. 0004 · Date: October 2, 2026
  • Received by: Lucía Fernández, ID No. 63.222.111
  • Paid by: María Gómez, ID No. 52.333.444
  • Description: making of an evening dress
  • Total agreed amount: COP 1,000,000
  • Advance received: COP 500,000 (five hundred thousand Colombian pesos), equal to 50%
  • Remaining balance: COP 500,000, payable on delivery
  • Payment method: bank transfer

Sample wording: “Received from María Gómez the sum of COP 500,000 as a 50% advance for making a dress, with a remaining balance of COP 500,000 on delivery.”

Conditions worth writing down

  • Whether the advance is refundable or not, and in which cases.
  • The delivery or completion date of the work.
  • What happens if either party cancels.
  • How and when the balance will be paid.

The conditions are agreed between the parties and, in some cases, your country's law sets rules; if in doubt, consult a lawyer.

Common mistakes

  • Not writing the remaining balance.
  • Describing the purpose vaguely.
  • Not keeping a signed copy.
  • Not stating the total agreed amount, only the advance.
  • Forgetting to issue a new receipt when the balance is paid.

Frequently asked questions

Can an advance be refunded?

It depends on what the parties agreed. That is why it is wise to put the conditions in writing, for example whether it is refundable.

Does it work for partial payments toward a debt?

Yes. Write the description as a partial payment, state the amount and the remaining balance, and both parties keep a copy.

How much should the advance be?

There is no fixed percentage: the parties agree on it depending on the type of work or product. What matters is that the amount and the balance are written down.

Should I issue another receipt when the balance is paid?

Yes. A new receipt records that the balance was paid and that nothing remains outstanding.

Who should sign the advance payment receipt?

The person who receives the money. Both parties should keep a copy.
